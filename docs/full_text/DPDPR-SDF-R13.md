**Rule 13 — Additional obligations of Significant Data Fiduciary**

(1) Once every 12 months, undertake a Data Protection Impact Assessment and audit.

(2) Furnish the Board a report with significant observations from that assessment/audit.

(3) Exercise due diligence to verify that its technical measures (including algorithmic software) for hosting/display/upload/modification/publishing/transmission/storage/update/sharing of personal data are not likely to pose a risk to Data Principals' rights.

(4) Ensure that personal data specified by the Central Government (on a constituted committee's recommendation) is processed subject to a restriction against transferring it, or related traffic data, outside India.
