**Section 29 — Appeal to Appellate Tribunal**

(1) Any person aggrieved by a Board order/direction may appeal to the Appellate Tribunal.

(2) Filed within 60 days of the order, in prescribed form/manner with prescribed fee.

(3) The Tribunal may condone delay for sufficient cause.

(4) After a hearing, the Tribunal may confirm, modify, or set aside the order.

(5) A copy of every order goes to the Board and the parties.

(6)-(7) The Tribunal shall endeavour to dispose of appeals within 6 months, recording reasons if it cannot.

(8)-(9) Procedure and further-appeal provisions follow the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 1997.

(10) The Tribunal functions as a digital office as far as practicable.
