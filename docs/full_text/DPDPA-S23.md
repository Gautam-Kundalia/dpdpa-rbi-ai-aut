**Section 23 — Proceedings of Board**

(1) Meeting/business procedure (including digital means) and order/direction authentication shall be as prescribed (see Rule 19).

(2) No Board act/proceeding is invalid merely due to a vacancy, constitution defect, appointment defect, or non-merits-affecting procedural irregularity.

(3) If the Chairperson is unable to act (absence/illness/other cause), the senior-most Member discharges her functions until she resumes.
