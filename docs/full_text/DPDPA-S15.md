**Section 15 — Duties of Data Principal**

A Data Principal shall: (a) comply with applicable laws while exercising Act rights; (b) not impersonate another person; (c) not suppress material information on State-issued documents/identifiers; (d) not register a false or frivolous grievance/complaint; and (e) furnish only verifiably authentic information when exercising correction/erasure rights.
