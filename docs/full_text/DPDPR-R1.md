**Rule 1 — Short title and commencement**

(1) These rules may be called the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

(2) Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21 come into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette (13 Nov 2025).

(3) Rule 4 comes into force one year after the date of publication (13 Nov 2026).

(4) Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 come into force eighteen months after the date of publication (13 May 2027).
