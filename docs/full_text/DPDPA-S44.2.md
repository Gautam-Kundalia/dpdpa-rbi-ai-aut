**Section 44(2) — Amendments to the Information Technology Act, 2000**

(a) Omits section 43A of the IT Act (the prior 'compensation for failure to protect data' provision — superseded by this Act's own breach/penalty regime).

(b) Inserts a reference to this Act alongside the Patents Act, 1970 in the proviso to IT Act section 81.

(c) Omits clause (ob) of IT Act section 87(2).
