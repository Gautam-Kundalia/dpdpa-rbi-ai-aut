**Section 10 — Additional obligations of Significant Data Fiduciary**

(1) The Central Government may notify a Data Fiduciary/class as Significant, based on factors including volume/sensitivity of data processed, risk to Data Principal rights, sovereignty/integrity impact, risk to electoral democracy, State security, and public order.

(2) A Significant Data Fiduciary shall: (a) appoint a Data Protection Officer — India-based, responsible to the Board of Directors, and the grievance point of contact; (b) appoint an independent data auditor; and (c) undertake periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments, periodic audits, and other prescribed measures.
