**Second Schedule (see Rules 5(1) and 16) — Standards for State processing (s.7(b)) and research/archiving/statistical processing (s.17(2)(b))**

Requires implementation of appropriate technical/organisational measures ensuring: (a) lawful processing; (b) processing limited to the specified use/purpose; (c) data minimisation; (d) reasonable efforts for completeness/accuracy/consistency; (e) retention limited to what's needed for the purpose or legal compliance; (f) reasonable security safeguards; (g) for s.7(b) processing specifically, giving the Data Principal intimation plus contact information, a communication link for exercising rights, and consistency with other applicable government policy/law; and (h) accountability of whoever determines the purpose and means of processing.
