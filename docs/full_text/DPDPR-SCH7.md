**Seventh Schedule (see Rules 8(3) and 23(1)) — Purposes and authorised persons**

1. Use of personal data by the State/instrumentality in the interest of India's sovereignty/integrity or State security — authorised person: an officer designated under s.17(2)(a) of the Act.

2. Use for performing a function under any law, or disclosing information to fulfil a legal obligation — authorised person: as authorised under the applicable law.

3. Assessing whether to notify a Data Fiduciary/class as a Significant Data Fiduciary — authorised person: an officer in MeitY designated by the Secretary in charge of that Ministry.
