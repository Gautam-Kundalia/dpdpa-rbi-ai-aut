**Section 12 — Right to correction and erasure of personal data**

(1) Right to correction, completion, updating and erasure for consented processing, per applicable law.

(2) On request, the Data Fiduciary shall correct inaccurate/misleading data, complete incomplete data, and update it.

(3) On an erasure request, the Data Fiduciary shall erase the data unless retention is necessary for the specified purpose or legal compliance.
