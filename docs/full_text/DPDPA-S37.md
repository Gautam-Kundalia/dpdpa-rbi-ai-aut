**Section 37 — Power of Central Government to issue directions**

(1) On a written Board reference intimating two-or-more penalty instances against a Data Fiduciary and advising public-interest blocking, the Central Government (after a hearing and recorded reasons) may direct blocking of public access to the relevant computer resource.

(2) Every intermediary receiving such a direction must comply.

(3) "Computer resource", "information" and "intermediary" take their meaning from the Information Technology Act, 2000.
