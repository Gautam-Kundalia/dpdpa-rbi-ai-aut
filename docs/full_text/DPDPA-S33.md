**Section 33 — Penalties**

(1) On concluding an inquiry that a breach is significant, the Board may, after a hearing, impose the monetary penalty specified in the Schedule (see DPDPA-SCHED).

(2) In setting the amount, the Board considers: breach nature/gravity/duration; type/nature of personal data affected; repetitive nature; whether the person gained or avoided loss; mitigation action taken and its timeliness/effectiveness; proportionality and deterrent effect; and likely impact on the person.
