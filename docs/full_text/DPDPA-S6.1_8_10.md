**Section 6(1)-(8),(10) — Consent**

(1) Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with clear affirmative action, limited to the personal data necessary for the specified purpose.

(2) Any part of a consent that infringes the Act, rules, or other law is invalid to that extent.

(3) Consent requests must be in clear, plain language, offer an Eighth Schedule language option, and give Data Protection Officer / authorised-person contact details.

(4) The Data Principal has the right to withdraw consent at any time, as easily as it was given.

(5) Withdrawal consequences are borne by the Data Principal; withdrawal doesn't affect the legality of prior processing.

(6) On withdrawal, the Data Fiduciary shall, within a reasonable time, cease (and cause its Data Processors to cease) processing, unless otherwise required/authorised by law.

(7) Consent may be given/managed/reviewed/withdrawn through a Consent Manager.

(8) The Consent Manager is accountable to the Data Principal and acts on her behalf.

(10) The Data Fiduciary bears the burden of proving valid notice and consent in any proceeding.
