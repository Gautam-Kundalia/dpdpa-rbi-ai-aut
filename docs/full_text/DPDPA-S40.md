**Section 40 — Power to make rules**

(1) The Central Government may, by notification (subject to prior publication), make rules not inconsistent with the Act to carry out its purposes.

(2) Without limiting the above, such rules may cover 26 listed matters, including: notice content (5(1)-(2)); Consent Manager accountability/obligations (6(8)) and registration (6(9)); the subsidy/benefit/service/licence/permit list for s.7(b); breach intimation form/manner (8(6)); the specified-purpose time period (8(8)); DPO contact publication (8(9)); verifiable consent manner (9(1)); child-processing exempt classes/purposes (9(4)); DPIA process details (10(2)(c)(i)); other SDF measures (10(2)(c)(iii)); access-request manner (11(1)); erasure-request manner (12(3)); grievance response period (13(2)); nomination manner (14(1)); research/archiving/statistical standards (17(2)(b)); Board Chairperson/Member appointment manner (19(2)), salary/terms (20(1)); order authentication manner (23(1)); Board officer/employee terms (24); Board techno-legal measures (28(1)) and other inquiry matters (28(7)(d)); appeal form/manner/fee (29(2)) and procedure (29(8)); and any other prescribable matter.

This is the legal basis for the DPDP Rules, 2025 as a whole (see DPDPR-* provisions).
