**Section 7 — Certain legitimate uses**

A Data Fiduciary may process personal data without separate consent for: (a) the specified purpose the Data Principal voluntarily provided data for, absent an indicated objection; (b) State/instrumentality provision of a prescribed subsidy/benefit/service/certificate/licence/permit; (c) State performance of a legal function or in the interest of India's sovereignty/security; (d) fulfilling a legal disclosure obligation to the State; (e) compliance with a judgment/decree/order; (f) responding to a medical emergency; (g) providing medical treatment during an epidemic/public health threat; (h) safety measures during a disaster or breakdown of public order; (i) employment purposes or safeguarding the employer from loss (e.g. trade secrets, corporate espionage prevention).
