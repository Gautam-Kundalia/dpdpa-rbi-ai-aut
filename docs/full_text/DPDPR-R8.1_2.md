**Rule 8(1)-(2) — Time period for specified purpose to be deemed as no longer being served**

(1) A Data Fiduciary of a class and processing for purposes specified in the Third Schedule shall erase personal data unless retention is required by law, or for the Third Schedule time period, if the Data Principal neither approaches the Data Fiduciary for the specified purpose nor exercises her rights.

(2) At least 48 hours before erasure, the Data Fiduciary shall inform the Data Principal that the data will be erased on completion of the period, unless she logs in / contacts the Data Fiduciary / exercises her rights.
