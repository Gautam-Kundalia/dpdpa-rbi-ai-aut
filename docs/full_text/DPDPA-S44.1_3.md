**Section 44(1),(3) — Amendments to certain Acts**

(1) Amends section 14(c) of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 1997 to add the Appellate Tribunal under this Act (alongside the IT Act and Airports Economic Regulatory Authority Act tribunals) to the definition of the TRAI Appellate Tribunal's remit.

(3) Amends section 8(1)(j) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, redefining the personal-information RTI exemption clause to simply read: "information which relates to personal information" (removing the prior public-interest balancing test language for personal information specifically).
