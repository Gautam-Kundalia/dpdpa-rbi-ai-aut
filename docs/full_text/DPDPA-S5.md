**Section 5 — Notice**

(1) Every consent request under section 6 shall be accompanied or preceded by a notice informing the Data Principal of: (i) the personal data and purpose of processing; (ii) how she may exercise her rights under section 6(4) and section 13; and (iii) how she may complain to the Board.

(2) Where consent was given before the Act's commencement, the Data Fiduciary shall, as soon as reasonably practicable, give an equivalent notice — and may continue processing until the Data Principal withdraws consent.

(3) The Data Fiduciary shall give the Data Principal the option to access the notice in English or any language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.
