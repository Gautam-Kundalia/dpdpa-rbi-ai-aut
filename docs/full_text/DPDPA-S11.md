**Section 11 — Right to access information about personal data**

(1) A Data Principal may request: (a) a summary of her personal data and processing activities; (b) identities of other Data Fiduciaries/Processors it's been shared with, with a description of what was shared; and (c) other prescribed information.

(2) Clauses (b) and (c) don't apply where sharing was pursuant to a written request from another authorised Data Fiduciary for offence/cyber-incident prevention, detection, investigation, prosecution or punishment.
