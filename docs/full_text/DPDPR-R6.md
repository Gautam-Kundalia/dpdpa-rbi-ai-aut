**Rule 6 — Reasonable security safeguards**

(1) A Data Fiduciary shall protect personal data in its possession/control, including processing by a Data Processor on its behalf, through measures including at minimum:
(a) data security measures such as encryption, obfuscation, masking, or virtual tokens;
(b) access controls over computer resources used for processing;
(c) visibility via logs, monitoring and review to detect/investigate/remediate unauthorised access;
(d) continuity measures (e.g. data backups) if confidentiality/integrity/availability is compromised;
(e) retention of such logs and personal data for at least one year, unless a law requires otherwise;
(f) appropriate contractual provisions with Data Processors; and
(g) appropriate technical and organisational measures for effective observance of safeguards.
