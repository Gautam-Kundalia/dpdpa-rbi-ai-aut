**Section 21 — Disqualifications for appointment and continuation as Chairperson and Members of Board**

(1) Disqualified if: (a) adjudged insolvent; (b) convicted of a moral-turpitude offence; (c) physically/mentally incapable; (d) has a prejudicial financial/other interest; or (e) has abused her position prejudicially to public interest.

(2) No removal without an opportunity of being heard.
