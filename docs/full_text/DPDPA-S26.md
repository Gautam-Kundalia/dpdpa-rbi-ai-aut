**Section 26 — Powers of Chairperson**

The Chairperson: (a) exercises general superintendence and direction over Board administrative matters; (b) may authorise an officer to scrutinise intimations/complaints/references/correspondence; and (c) may authorise a Member or group of Members to perform Board functions/conduct proceedings and allocate work among them.
