**Section 28 — Procedure to be followed by Board**

(1) The Board functions as an independent, digital-by-design body for complaint receipt, allocation, hearing, and decisions.

(2)-(6) On receiving an intimation/complaint/reference/direction, the Board determines if there are sufficient grounds, closes proceedings with recorded reasons if not, or inquires (following natural justice, with recorded reasons) if so.

(7) The Board has civil-court-equivalent powers: summoning/examining on oath, receiving affidavit evidence, requiring document discovery/production, inspecting data/documents, and other prescribed matters.

(8) The Board/its officers cannot block premises access or seize equipment affecting day-to-day functioning.

(9) The Board may requisition police/government officer assistance.

(10) The Board may issue interim orders during inquiry, after a hearing, with recorded reasons.

(11) On completion, after a hearing, the Board may close proceedings or proceed under section 33.

(12) The Board may warn or impose costs on a complainant it finds to be false or frivolous.
