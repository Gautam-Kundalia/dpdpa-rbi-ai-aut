**Rule 5 — Processing of personal data for provision or issue of subsidy, benefit, service, certificate, licence or permit by State and its instrumentalities**

(1) Such processing shall follow the standards specified in the Second Schedule.

(2) Clarifies that references to a subsidy/benefit/service/etc. provided or issued 'under law', 'under policy', or 'using public funds' cover State/State-instrumentality action under any applicable law, executive policy, or expenditure from the Consolidated Fund/public account of India or a State, respectively.
