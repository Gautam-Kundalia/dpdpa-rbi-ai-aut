**Rule 15 — Transfer of personal data outside the territory of India**

Personal data processed under the Act may be transferred outside India, subject to the Data Fiduciary meeting such requirements as the Central Government may, by general or special order, specify in respect of making such data available to any foreign State, or to any person/entity/agency under the control of such a State.
