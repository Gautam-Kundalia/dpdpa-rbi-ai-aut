**Section 13 — Right of grievance redressal**

(1) Right to readily available grievance redressal for any act/omission of the Data Fiduciary/Consent Manager regarding her personal data or rights.

(2) Response must be given within a prescribed period from receipt.

(3) The Data Principal must exhaust this route before approaching the Board.
