**Section 19 — Composition and qualifications for appointment of Chairperson and Members**

(1) The Board consists of a Chairperson and such number of Members as notified.

(2) Appointed by the Central Government in a prescribed manner (see Rule 17).

(3) Must be a person of ability, integrity and standing with special knowledge/practical experience in data governance, administration, dispute resolution, ICT, digital economy, law, regulation, techno-regulation, or another useful field — at least one Member must be a legal expert.
