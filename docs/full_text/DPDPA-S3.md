**Section 3 — Application of Act**

Subject to the Act's provisions, it shall:
(a) apply to processing of digital personal data within India where the data is collected in digital form, or in non-digital form and digitised subsequently;
(b) also apply to processing outside India, if connected with offering goods or services to Data Principals within India;
(c) not apply to personal data processed by an individual for personal or domestic purposes, or personal data made publicly available by the Data Principal herself or by someone under a legal obligation to make it public.

**Illustration:** An individual who publicly posts her own personal data while blogging is not covered by the Act for that data.
