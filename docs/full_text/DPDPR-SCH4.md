**Fourth Schedule (see Rule 12) — Exemptions from Section 9(1)/(3) of the Act for processing a child's data**

**Part A — Exempted classes** (conditions attached): clinical/mental-health establishments and healthcare professionals (processing limited to health services necessary for the child's health protection); allied healthcare professionals (limited to supporting a recommended treatment/referral plan); educational institutions (limited to tracking/monitoring for educational activities or child safety); crèche/childcare individuals (limited to safety-related tracking/monitoring); transport providers engaged by such institutions (limited to location tracking during transit).

**Part B — Exempted purposes** (conditions attached): exercising a legal power/duty in a child's interest; providing a government subsidy/benefit/service/licence/permit in a child's interest; creating an email-only user account; determining a child's real-time location for safety; ensuring harmful information/services/ads don't reach a child; and the Data Fiduciary's own confirmation that a Data Principal is not a child (Rule 10 due diligence).
