**First Schedule (see Rule 4) — Consent Manager registration & obligations**

**Part A — Registration conditions** (abridged): Indian-incorporated company; sufficient technical/operational/financial capacity; sound financial condition; net worth ≥ ₹2 crore; adequate business volume/capital structure; fit-and-proper directors/KMP/senior management; MOA/AOA provisions locking in Part B items 9-10 obligations (amendable only with Board approval); operations in Data Principals' interests; independent certification of platform's data-protection standards compliance.

**Part B — Obligations** (abridged, 13 items): enable consent to be given/managed/reviewed/withdrawn directly or via another onboarded Data Fiduciary; ensure data routed through the platform isn't readable by the Consent Manager itself; maintain records of consents given/denied/withdrawn, notices, and data-sharing events, retained ≥7 years; give Data Principals access to these records in machine-readable form; maintain a primary website/app; no sub-contracting of statutory obligations; reasonable security safeguards; act in a fiduciary capacity; avoid conflicts of interest with Data Fiduciaries; publish ownership/governance transparency information; maintain effective audit mechanisms reporting to the Board; no change of control without prior Board approval.
