**Section 17 — Exemptions**

(1) Most of Chapter II (except s.8(1) and (5)), Chapter III, and section 16 do not apply where processing is: (a) necessary for enforcing a legal right/claim; (b) by a court/tribunal/body performing a judicial, quasi-judicial, regulatory or supervisory function; (c) for prevention/detection/investigation/prosecution of an offence; (d) of non-Indian Data Principals under a foreign contract; (e) necessary for a court/tribunal-approved merger/demerger/reconstruction; or (f) for ascertaining a loan defaulter's financial information.

(2) The Act doesn't apply to processing by a Central-Government-notified security/sovereignty/public-order instrumentality, or to research/archiving/statistical processing meeting prescribed standards (Second Schedule of the Rules).

(3) The Central Government may exempt certain Data Fiduciaries (including startups) from sections 5, 8(3)&(7), 10, and 11.

(4) For State/instrumentality processing, s.8(7) and s.12(3) (and s.12(2) where the purpose doesn't involve a decision affecting the Data Principal) don't apply.

(5) The Central Government may, within 5 years of Act commencement, exempt Data Fiduciaries from any provision for a specified period.
