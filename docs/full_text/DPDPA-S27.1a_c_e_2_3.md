**Section 27(1)(a)-(c),(e),(2),(3) — Powers and functions of Board**

(1) The Board shall: (a) on breach intimation under s.8(6), direct urgent remedial/mitigation measures, inquire, and impose penalty; (b) on a Data Principal complaint, Central/State Government reference, or court direction, inquire into a breach of Data Fiduciary obligations and impose penalty; (c) on a complaint about a Consent Manager's breach of obligations, inquire and impose penalty; (e) on a Central Government reference about an intermediary's breach of s.37(2), inquire and impose penalty.

(2) The Board may, after a hearing and recorded reasons, issue binding directions necessary for effective discharge of its functions.

(3) The Board may, on representation by an affected person or government reference, modify, suspend, withdraw or cancel a direction, with such conditions as it sees fit.
