**Rule 4 — Registration and obligations of Consent Manager**

(1) An applicant meeting First Schedule Part A conditions may apply to the Board for registration as a Consent Manager.

(2) On application, the Board may inquire and either register the applicant (publishing particulars on its website) or reject the application with reasons.

(3) Obligations of a Consent Manager are set out in First Schedule Part B.

(4) If the Board finds non-adherence, it may, after a hearing, direct corrective measures.

(5) The Board may, after a hearing and for recorded reasons, suspend or cancel registration and give directions to protect Data Principals' interests.

(6) The Board may require the Consent Manager to furnish information it calls for.
