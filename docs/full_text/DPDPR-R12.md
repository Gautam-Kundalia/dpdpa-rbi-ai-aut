**Rule 12 — Exemptions from certain obligations applicable to processing of personal data of child**

(1) Section 9(1) and (3) of the Act do not apply to processing of a child's personal data by the classes of Data Fiduciary specified in Fourth Schedule Part A, subject to the conditions specified there.

(2) Section 9(1) and (3) also do not apply for the purposes specified in Fourth Schedule Part B, subject to conditions specified there.
