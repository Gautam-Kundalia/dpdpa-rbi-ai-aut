**Rule 11 — Verifiable consent for processing of personal data of person with disability who has lawful guardian**

(1) A Data Fiduciary obtaining verifiable consent from a self-identified lawful guardian shall exercise due diligence to verify the guardian was appointed by a court, a designated authority, or a local level committee under the law applicable to guardianship.

(2) Defines 'designated authority' (under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016), 'law applicable to guardianship' (referencing the RPwD Act, 2016 and the National Trust Act, 1999), 'local level committee', and 'person with disability'.
