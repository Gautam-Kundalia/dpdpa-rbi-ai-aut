**Rule 14 — Rights of Data Principals**

(1) The Data Fiduciary (and Consent Manager, where applicable) shall prominently publish the means for making a rights-exercise request, and any identifying particulars required.

(2) A Data Principal may request the Data Fiduciary she previously consented to, using the required means.

(3) Every Data Fiduciary/Consent Manager shall publish its grievance redressal system, responding within a reasonable period not exceeding 90 days, with appropriate technical/organisational measures for effectiveness.

(4) A Data Principal may nominate one or more individuals to exercise her rights, per the Data Fiduciary's terms of service and applicable law.
