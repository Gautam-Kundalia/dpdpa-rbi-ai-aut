**Section 30 — Orders passed by Appellate Tribunal to be executable as decree**

(1) An Appellate Tribunal order is executable by it as a civil-court decree, with all corresponding powers.

(2) The Tribunal may instead transmit the order to a local civil court for execution as its own decree.
