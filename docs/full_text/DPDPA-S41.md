**Section 41 — Laying of rules and certain notifications**

Every rule made, and every notification issued under sections 16 and 42, shall be laid before each House of Parliament for a total of 30 days (in one or more sessions). If both Houses agree to modify or annul it before the following session ends, it takes effect only in modified form or ceases to have effect — without prejudice to anything already validly done under it.
