**Third Schedule (see Rule 8(1)) — Classes of Data Fiduciary and erasure time periods**

Three classes, each subject to a 3-year erasure clock (from the Data Principal's last approach for the specified purpose, or exercise of rights, or Rules commencement — whichever is latest), applicable to all purposes except enabling account access and enabling access to virtual tokens usable for money/goods/services:
1. E-commerce entity with ≥ 2 crore registered users in India.
2. Online gaming intermediary with ≥ 50 lakh registered users in India.
3. Social media intermediary with ≥ 2 crore registered users in India.
