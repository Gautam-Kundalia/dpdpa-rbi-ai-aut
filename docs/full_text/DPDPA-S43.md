**Section 43 — Power to remove difficulties**

(1) If a difficulty arises in giving effect to the Act, the Central Government may, by Gazette order, make necessary provisions not inconsistent with the Act.

(2) No such order may be made after 3 years from Act commencement.

(3) Every such order shall be laid before each House of Parliament.
