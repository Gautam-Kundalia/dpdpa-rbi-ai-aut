**Rule 3 — Notice given by Data Fiduciary to Data Principal**

The notice shall:
(a) be presented and understandable independently of any other information made available by the Data Fiduciary;
(b) give, in clear and plain language, a fair account of details necessary for specific and informed consent, including at minimum an itemised description of the personal data and the specified purpose(s) of processing and goods/services/uses enabled by it; and
(c) give the communication link to the Data Fiduciary's website/app, and describe other means, if any, by which the Data Principal may withdraw consent (as easily as it was given), exercise her rights under the Act, and complain to the Board.
