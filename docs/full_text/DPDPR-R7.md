**Rule 7 — Intimation of personal data breach**

(1) On becoming aware of a breach, the Data Fiduciary shall, without delay, inform each affected Data Principal of: the breach's nature/extent/timing; likely consequences to her; mitigation measures taken/underway; safety measures she may take; and contact information for queries.

(2) The Data Fiduciary shall also inform the Board:
(a) without delay, a description of the breach (nature, extent, timing, location, likely impact); and
(b) within 72 hours of becoming aware (or a longer period the Board allows in writing), updated/detailed information, facts on causes/circumstances, mitigation measures, findings on the person who caused the breach, remedial measures taken, and a report on intimations given to affected Data Principals.
