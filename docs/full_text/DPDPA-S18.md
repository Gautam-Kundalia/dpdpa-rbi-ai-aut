**Section 18 — Establishment of Board**

(1) With effect from a notified date, the Data Protection Board of India is established.

(2) The Board is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, able to acquire/hold/dispose of property and to sue/be sued.

(3) Headquarters at a Central-Government-notified place.
