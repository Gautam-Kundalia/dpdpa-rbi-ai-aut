**Section 8 — General obligations of Data Fiduciary**

(1) The Data Fiduciary is responsible for Act compliance regardless of any contrary agreement or Data Principal default.

(2) A Data Processor may only be engaged under a valid contract.

(3) Where processed data will be used for a decision affecting the Data Principal, or disclosed to another Data Fiduciary, completeness/accuracy/consistency must be ensured.

(4) Appropriate technical/organisational measures shall ensure effective Act compliance.

(5) Reasonable security safeguards shall be taken to prevent personal data breach.

(6) On a breach, the Data Fiduciary shall intimate the Board and each affected Data Principal, in prescribed form and manner.

(7) Unless retention is legally necessary, the Data Fiduciary shall erase personal data on consent withdrawal or once the specified purpose is no longer served (whichever is earlier), and cause its Data Processor to do the same.

(8) The purpose is deemed no longer served if the Data Principal neither approaches the Data Fiduciary nor exercises her rights for a prescribed time period (which can vary by Data Fiduciary class/purpose).

(9) The Data Fiduciary shall publish DPO/contact business information for Data Principal questions.

(10) An effective grievance redressal mechanism must be established.

(11) A Data Principal is deemed not to have approached the Data Fiduciary during any period she initiated no contact, in person or electronically/physically.
