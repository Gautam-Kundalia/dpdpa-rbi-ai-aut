**Section 9 — Processing of personal data of children**

(1) Verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian must be obtained before processing a child's (or disabled person's, with lawful guardian) personal data.

(2) No processing likely to cause detrimental effect on a child's wellbeing.

(3) No tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children.

(4) Sub-sections (1) and (3) don't apply to prescribed classes of Data Fiduciary/purposes/conditions (see Rule 12 and Fourth Schedule).

(5) The Central Government may notify a safe age threshold above which a verifiably-safe Data Fiduciary is exempt from (1) and (3).
