# Schedule (Act) — Penalties

**Schedule (see section 33(1)) — Penalties**

| Breach | Penalty (may extend to) |
|---|---|
| Failure to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a breach (s.8(5)) | ₹250 crore |
| Failure to notify the Board/affected Data Principals of a breach (s.8(6)) | ₹200 crore |
| Breach of additional obligations relating to children (s.9) | ₹200 crore |
| Breach of additional obligations of a Significant Data Fiduciary (s.10) | ₹150 crore |
| Breach of Data Principal duties (s.15) | ₹10,000 |
| Breach of a voluntary undertaking accepted by the Board (s.32) | Up to whatever applied to the original proceeding |
| Breach of any other provision of the Act or Rules | ₹50 crore |
