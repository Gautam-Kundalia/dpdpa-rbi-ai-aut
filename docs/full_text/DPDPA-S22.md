**Section 22 — Resignation by Members and filling of vacancy**

(1) Resignation is effective on Government permission, expiry of 3 months' notice, a successor entering office, or term expiry — whichever is earliest.

(2) Vacancies are filled by fresh appointment.

(3) For one year after leaving office, prior Central Government approval is needed before accepting employment, and any subsequent employment with a Data Fiduciary they handled proceedings against must be disclosed.
